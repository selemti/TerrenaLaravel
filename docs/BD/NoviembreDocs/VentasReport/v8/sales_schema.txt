6) Notas
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- PG 9.5: forzar casts `::numeric`, `::text` en vistas para evitar "unknown".
- `folio_date` puede estar NULL; usar COALESCE(closing_date::date, create_date::date).
- `fn_correct_drawer_report(date)` devuelve: terminal_id, original_total_revenue, corrected_neto_tickets, adjustment.
- Para cajón vs efectivo se usa `f_diag_drawer_vs_cash_transactions_on(date)` y wrapper `vw_diag_drawer_vs_cash_transactions` (hoy).
